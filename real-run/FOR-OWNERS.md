# Try the Find the Idea tool (about 20 minutes)

You answer questions about your own business. At the end you get one idea and one test to run this week.
Or it tells you there isn't enough evidence yet and what to go count. Either answer is useful.

## Before you start
- Open ChatGPT or Claude on a computer. Turn on web search if you see the option.
- Have these close by: your Google reviews, your phone's call log, and a rough idea of quotes you lost lately.

## Steps
1. Open this link and copy everything on the page:
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jdvanleenen-sys/find-the-idea/main/find-the-idea-prompt.md
2. Paste it into a new chat and send it.
3. Answer its questions one at a time. Short answers are fine. If you don't know a number, say so.
4. Use counts, not names. Don't paste customer names, phone numbers or emails.
5. When it finishes, copy the whole chat and send it back to me. Delete any names you spot first.

## Then answer three questions
1. Did the test it gave you make sense?
2. Will you run it this week? If not, why not?
3. What confused you or felt wrong?

## What happens to your chat
I read every one to make the tool better. It stays private: it is never posted, and names are taken out
before I keep it. If you'd rather I didn't keep it at all, tell me and I'll delete it after reading.
